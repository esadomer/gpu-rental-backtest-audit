## Scientific positioning

The contribution is an auditable forecastability and measurement study of public GPU rental catalogs. It is not the first GPU forecasting paper, the first historical catalog reconstruction, or a demonstrated profitable strategy. Persistence beating a fixed model menu is an honest result, but does not by itself establish novelty or strong market-wide inference. The distinctive evidence is the combination of source timing, provider aliases, repeated members, observable service identity and missing-target accounting.

A renewed inspection of Mittal's current public tree and all 44 Python scripts confirms substantial overlap in the underlying measurement concerns. The current revision equals the pinned release. No equivalent rolling future-rental forecast pipeline or matched forecast-loss diagnostic was found in the inspected files, but the full described manuscript was not located. See [the overlap audit](literature_overlap.md); do not infer the manuscript lacks additional analysis.
